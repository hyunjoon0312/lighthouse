The bundled FFDNet.mlmodel is a Lighthouse conversion of the official
ffdnet_color_clip weights. It contains the 12 convolution layers and the ReLU
activations between them. The caller supplies an FP32 [13,160,160] tensor:
12 pixel-unshuffled sRGB planes in channel order 4*c + 2*dy + dx, followed by
one constant sigma plane. The FP32 [12,160,160] output is the directly restored
RGB tile in the same plane order; it is not a residual.

Conversion toolchain: Python 3.11, torch 2.7.0, coremltools 9.0, numpy 2.4.6.
Reproduction and parity checks: scripts/convert-denoise-model.py
